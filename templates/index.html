ABOUT FLIGHTAURA

Better context for every journey.

FlightAura is a passenger-review intelligence application built around historical airline experiences. It combines natural language processing, machine learning and evidence retrieval to make large collections of passenger feedback easier to explore.

Its findings reflect the dataset available to the application and should be interpreted as historical passenger feedback rather than an official airline rating.
